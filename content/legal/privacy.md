# Privacy Notice

**Version 2026-10-02.** [Български](/legal/privacy-bg)

## Who we are and how to reach us

LiberHack is organised by the LiberHack team. For anything about your data,
email **[contact@liberhack.org](mailto:contact@liberhack.org)**. We reply
within one month.

## What we collect and why

| What | Why | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email, password (stored hashed) | Your account | Art. 6(1)(b), taking part in the event you signed up for |
| Skills, experience level, team, project links | Running the event: teams, mentors, judging | Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Agreement to the Регламент and Code of Conduct | Event rules | Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Food choice: none, vegetarian, vegan or other | Catering | Art. 6(1)(b) |
| Optional short food note (e.g. "no nuts"), offered only once a deletion period for catering data is set | Catering | Your **explicit consent**, Art. 9(2)(a), because it can reveal health or religion |
| Optional: sharing your profile with named organisations for jobs and internships | See "Jobs and internships" | Your **consent**, Art. 6(1)(a) |
| "Are you 18 or older?" (asked only if you say Yes to sharing) | We only share profiles of adults | Part of the same consent |
| Optional: emails about future LiberHack events | Telling you about later editions | Your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) |
| Optional: your name and team in the public archive | Showing past projects | Your consent; off unless you tick it |
| Optional: browser analytics | Learning which posters and posts bring people in | Your consent, Art. 6(1)(a), and Art. 4a of the Electronic Communications Act |
| A record of each of these choices | Showing what you chose and when | Art. 6(1)(c) and (f): demonstrating consent (Art. 7(1)) |

We do not sell data and use no advertising trackers.

## Jobs and internships (optional)

When you register, we ask separately for each named organisation whether we
may share your **name, email, skills and experience level** with it so it can
contact you about jobs and internships. **No is a full answer and changes
nothing about your participation.** Nothing is preselected.

- We share a profile only with an organisation you said Yes to, and only if
  you told us you are 18 or older.
- Your food choices and notes, analytics, password and account data are never
  shared.
- If an organisation is added later, nothing is shared with it unless you say
  Yes to it in [your privacy settings](/ops/privacy).
- You can change any answer to No in [your privacy settings](/ops/privacy) at
  any time. You are then left out of every later export. Each organisation
  that already received your profile uses it independently, under its own
  privacy policy. We will tell you which organisations received it and when,
  and pass your request on to them. We cannot pull back a copy they already
  have.
- The organisations are listed by legal name on the registration form and in
  your privacy settings.

## Browser analytics (optional)

This feature is currently **switched off**. When it is switched on, it works
like this, and only if you click **Allow analytics**:

- We set a random ID in a secure cookie that page scripts cannot read. It lasts
  exactly **30 days** and is never extended. After that we ask again.
- From the moment you allow it, we record up to four things:
  - that you opened the site, with the poster or post link you are on at that
    moment, or the kind of site you came from (e.g. "Instagram");
  - that you clicked "register";
  - that you started the form;
  - that your registration was completed.

  Times are kept to the hour. **Nothing from before you clicked Allow is
  recorded.**
- We do not store your name, email, form answers, IP address, full web
  addresses or a device fingerprint with it, and we do not link it to your
  account.
- Individual events are deleted after **60 days** at most. Totals per day and
  per source are kept after that. They are made only once your ID can no
  longer be withdrawn, so a withdrawal always removes you from them too.
- **Reject analytics**: nothing is collected, and we will not ask again for
  180 days. Registering works the same either way.
- **Withdraw any time** with *Privacy settings* at the bottom of every page,
  without logging in. This deletes this browser's events and ID immediately.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Kept |
|---|---|
| Analytics ID and its events | ID: 30 days from your consent. Events: at most 60 days |
| Analytics totals per day and source | Kept; they contain no IDs |
| Account (name, email) | Until you or we delete it |
| Per-edition details (skills, experience, team) | Until you ask us to delete them. We have not yet set an automatic deletion date, and we will update this notice when we do |
| Food choice | Until you ask us to delete it. Food notes are only offered once an automatic deletion date is set, which this notice will state |
| Records of your choices | Until you delete your account. Superseded records may be removed earlier |
| Log of exports (which account IDs went to which organisation, and when) | Until we set a deletion period. It holds account IDs, not your details |
| Backups | Deleted records can survive in our provider's backups until those expire. If a backup is restored, we delete them again |

## Your rights

You can ask to **see**, **correct** or **delete** your data, to **restrict**
or **object to** its use, and to get a **copy** to take elsewhere. You can
**withdraw any consent** at any time; this does not affect what happened
before. Withdrawing or saying No never cancels your registration or anything
you get from taking part.

You can change most choices yourself in
[your privacy settings](/ops/privacy). For everything else, email
[contact@liberhack.org](mailto:contact@liberhack.org).

You can complain to the Bulgarian data protection authority:
**Комисия за защита на личните данни (КЗЛД)**, бул. „Проф. Цветан Лазаров“ 2,
София 1592, [www.cpdp.bg](https://www.cpdp.bg).

## Age

LiberHack is for people aged 14 to 25. We ask "Are you 18 or older?" only
when you say Yes to sharing your profile, and share nothing for anyone who
answers No. We never ask for a date of birth or ID document for analytics.

## Changes

When this notice changes in a way that affects what you agreed to, we ask you
again. Your earlier answers stay recorded against the version you saw.
