## Sponsor sharing

Taking part includes sharing your **name, email, skills and experience level**
with the sponsor organisations named on the registration form, for internship
and job recruitment. Your food choices, notes, analytics, password and account
data are never shared with sponsors.

- The form lists each organisation by name. Your confirmation is stored with
  that exact list. If a sponsor is added later, nothing is shared with it until
  you confirm the new list in **[your privacy settings](/ops/privacy)**.
- Each sponsor uses the data **independently**, as its own controller, under
  its own privacy policy. **[to confirm: each sponsor's privacy contact]**
- **[to confirm: the legal basis for making this sharing a condition of taking
  part. A documented legal assessment is required before sponsor exports are
  used.]**
- You can object to or ask us to stop sharing at any time by email. You are then
  left out of all later exports and keep your place at the event. Copies a
  sponsor already received are theirs to delete. We will tell you which sponsors
  received your data and when, and pass your request on to them.

## Browser analytics

Only if you click **Allow analytics** in the banner:

- We set a random ID in a secure, HttpOnly cookie for **30 days**. It is not
  extended. When it expires we ask again.
- We record up to four events: that you **opened the site** (with the poster or
  post link that brought you, or the type of site you came from, such as
  "Instagram"), **clicked "register"**, **started the form** and **finished
  registering**. Times are kept to the hour.
- We do **not** record your name, email, form answers, IP address, full web
  addresses or device fingerprint with it, and we do not link it to your account.
- Individual events are deleted after **60 days** at most. After that only
  totals remain, such as "12 registrations came from the FMI poster in week 3".
- If you click **Reject analytics**, nothing is collected and we will not ask
  again for 180 days. Registering works exactly the same either way.
- **Withdraw any time** with **Privacy settings** at the bottom of every page,
  no account needed. Withdrawing deletes this browser's events and ID straight
  away.

If you are under 14, please ask a parent or guardian before allowing analytics.

## Your rights

You can ask to **access**, **correct** or **delete** your data, **restrict** or
**object to** its use, get a **copy** to take elsewhere, and **withdraw any
consent** at any time without affecting what happened before. Withdrawing or
objecting never cancels your registration or anything you get from taking part.

Most choices can be changed yourself in **[your privacy settings](/ops/privacy)**.
For everything else, email us. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Bulgarian data protection authority:
**Комисия за защита на личните данни (КЗЛД)**, бул. „Проф. Цветан Лазаров“ 2,
София 1592, [www.cpdp.bg](https://www.cpdp.bg).
